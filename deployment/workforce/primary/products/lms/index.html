Questions

Can Meta lead ads come in automatically?

Yes. Leads from connected Facebook and Instagram lead forms arrive in the LMS on their own, with the source recorded, so nobody copies them from a spreadsheet.

Does my team need to change how they call?

No. Telecallers keep calling from their phones. The LMS shows who to call next and records what happened.

Can I bring my existing leads?

Yes. Existing leads can be imported, so the team starts with its full list.

What does it cost?

Plans start at ₹599 / user / month + GST, billed yearly. The 6-month plan is ₹699 and the 3-month plan from ₹999 / user / month + GST.

Are prices inclusive of GST?

No, GST at 18% is added.
