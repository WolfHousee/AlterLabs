Questions, answered

Is telephony usage included?

No. The role costs ₹7,999/month plus usage. Voice, telephony and third-party communication usage are billed separately.

Are these human employees?

Alter Workforce roles are software-based virtual employees operated through AlterLabs systems and integrations. They are not human employees. Your business retains human oversight and decision authority.

What stays under human control?

You set the scope, access permissions and approval rules. High-impact decisions, sensitive actions and exceptions go to your designated human owner.

What does business setup cost?

Business Setup costs ₹9,999 one-time. Setup covers the agreed roles, workflow mapping, access boundaries, supported integrations and review process.

Is communication usage included?

Voice, telephony and third-party communication usage are billed separately. Connected services and permitted actions are agreed during setup.
